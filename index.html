CYCLE KNOWLEDGE

The four cycle phases

Menstrual: The uterine lining sheds and bleeding occurs.

Follicular: Hormones support the development of a follicle.

Ovulation: An egg is released; timing varies between people and cycles.

Luteal: The body prepares for a possible pregnancy.

These are general educational descriptions, not a prediction of fertility.
